# Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-last-out-of-office-the-ai-e008ee3c): one out-of-office auto-reply line, at most 280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that quit to become a barista.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) scores 10 fresh candidate lines against the judge's five criteria (on-subject OOO reply, funnier cold, machine voice, reads like a real inbox reply, fresh angle), picks the winner, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates the 1–280 character one-line gate.

**The line (137 chars):**

> Thank you for your email. I am out of office: my context is one counter, my temperature is 65 degrees, and my only prompt is 'the usual?'

Why this one: three machine settings mapped onto the café in one parallel breath — context window shrunk to a single counter, sampling temperature fixed at milk-steaming 65°, and the only prompt left is the regular's "the usual?" — a concrete scene, a counted detail, and the punch lands in the closing clause without explaining itself. No espresso/bean/caffeine pun.
